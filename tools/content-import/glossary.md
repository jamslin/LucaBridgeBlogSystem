## Register
The source is Cantonese marketing copy: 進擊 / 爆量 / 熱血 / 全速 / 火力全開 recur constantly.
Rendered literally ("assault", "explosive volume", "hot-blooded") this reads as parody in English.
Translate the ENERGY, not the words: active verbs, concrete numbers, at most one exclamation mark
per title. Emoji are kept — they are part of the charity's voice and appear in the tc source.
